Terms & Conditions

Harvest Commit is a harvest-planning prototype operated by Clinton Imaro. These terms describe the invited SMS demonstration program.

SMS Terms

After reviewing the invitation at our SMS sign-up page, you may text START to +1 (682) 432-9774 to request recurring automated Harvest Commit messages. You must control the phone number you use. The coordinator must configure your number before plans can be sent; the prototype does not send plans to an unrestricted subscriber list.

Messages include proposed daily harvest quantities, inventory and field priorities, approval requests, plan updates, and responses to your approval or edit replies. Messages are non-marketing. Message frequency varies based on requested plans and replies. Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier’s charges remain your responsibility. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

Stopping and restarting messages

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. You may receive an opt-out confirmation, after which no further program messages will be sent unless you opt back in. Reply START to request enrollment again. An SMS enrollment confirmation does not approve a harvest plan.

Help

Reply HELP for program information. For assistance, text SUPPORT followed by your question to +1 (682) 432-9774. Clinton Imaro reviews support messages manually. Support and message delivery are not guaranteed to be immediate; this is not an emergency service.

Plans and delivery

Harvest Commit uses sample farm records in this demonstration. Review every quantity and assumption before approving. Replies must include the current plan code. Editing a plan requires new approval, and a customer’s incoming text requires review before it affects orders. The prototype does not determine crop safety or offer unsupervised agronomic advice. SMS depends on connectivity, provider availability, and carrier approval. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for information about phone numbers, message content, consent records, service providers, retention, and privacy requests. Do not send sensitive personal data through the SMS demo.
