Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Harvest Commit SMS prototype operated by Clinton Imaro and this program information website.

Information we collect

When you text the program, Harvest Commit and its messaging provider process your phone number, message text, timestamps, message identifiers, delivery status, and opt-in or opt-out requests. The application also stores submitted order details, proposed harvest plans, and approval or edit replies. The website itself has no sign-up form, advertising, or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the website.

How information is used

We use this information to send requested plans, record consent and opt-outs, match replies to the correct plan, review incoming messages, troubleshoot delivery, and respond to support or privacy requests. A text does not automatically change a customer order. A person must review and confirm it.

Service providers and sharing

Twilio and telecommunications carriers process messages to provide SMS delivery and opt-out controls. Vercel hosts the application, APIs, and these pages. Upstash provides the application database for farm records, incoming messages, consent state, and plan replies. These providers process data to operate the service. Authorized project operators can review the prototype’s messages and plans. Data may also be disclosed when legally required.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS consent is not transferred to other brands or unrelated messaging programs. The review screen initially uses a local parser. If an authorized operator chooses Extract with AI, the selected message text and known customer names are sent to OpenAI to suggest order details. Phone numbers are not sent as separate fields, but information included in the message text is processed. The operator must check the original message before applying any suggestion. This step does not approve orders or send texts. API requests use store: false; OpenAI may still retain processing records under its policies, so this is not a promise of zero retention.

Storage and choices

Application messages and plans are stored in the hosted Upstash database with workspace access controls. Browser copies support local drafts, and the operator keeps a migration backup on their computer. Moving a message to Trash hides it from the inbox and allows it to be restored; it does not erase the message or any reviewed order record. Twilio and carriers may retain their own processing records under their policies. Prototype records are retained while needed for the demo, support, and consent records; there is no automatic deletion schedule in this prototype. Contact the operator to request access, correction, or deletion. Some records may need to remain for opt-out enforcement or legal requirements.

Reply STOP to end program texts. Replying START requests enrollment again. Stopping texts does not erase earlier messages. Participation is optional, and SMS is not a confidential channel; do not send financial, medical, identity-document, or other sensitive information.

Contact
